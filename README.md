# Whisker Worlds

A 2D adventure platformer for mobile (iOS, Android) and later desktop. Two cats, Miso (an older calico) and Pepper (a young white and grey tom), explore their skyrise apartment and tumble into micro worlds hidden in the furniture, recovering the seven notes of a broken music box.
