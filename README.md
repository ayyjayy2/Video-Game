# Whisker Worlds

A third-person 3D adventure for mobile (iOS, Android) and later desktop. Two cats, Cappuccino (an older calico) and Asher (a young white and grey tom), explore their skyrise apartment and tumble into micro worlds hidden in the furniture, recovering the seven notes of a broken music box.

## Design

- `docs/design-bible.html`: the full design bible (characters, story, game structure, all mission designs, concept art mapping, camera and visual direction, controls and technical setup). Open it in any browser.
- `docs/art/`: web-sized crops and sheets used by the design bible.
- `docs/art/originals/`: the original concept art at full resolution.
